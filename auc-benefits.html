GitHub Education Verification

Once GitHub verifies your student status, GitHub Education becomes a gateway to many additional student software and development offers.

Student discounts often require more than simply typing an educational email address. Keep current digital copies of the following available:

  • Your AUC student ID.
  • An enrollment verification document.
  • A current transcript or other document showing active enrollment when appropriate.
  • Access to your active @aucegypt.edu email account.

Different services use different verification platforms and may require periodic re-verification.

If you do not want to activate dozens of services, these are among the highest-value benefits worth checking first:

  • Google AI Pro student eligibility — check the current Egypt student promotion.
  • GitHub Student Developer Pack — free for eligible verified students.
  • GitHub Copilot Student — check current verified-student eligibility.
  • Microsoft Office 365 — included through AUC.
  • Figma Professional — free for eligible education accounts.
  • Notion Plus Education — free for eligible university students.
  • Autodesk Education — free educational access to professional Autodesk software.
  • Azure for Students — includes student cloud credit.
  • JetBrains educational license — professional IDEs for eligible students.
  • MATLAB — check AUC's software access before purchasing.
  • SPSS and STATA — available within AUC's technology environment.
  • AUC Library databases — use them heavily while actively enrolled.
  • LinkedIn Learning — available through AUC.
  • AUC Sports Complex — take advantage of facilities you would otherwise pay for elsewhere.
  • Peer Tutoring — free academic support.
  • Writing and Communication Center — free academic and professional writing support.
  • Career Center — internships, career advising and employer connections.
  • Counseling services — confidential student support.
  • Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube student plans — check before paying standard subscription prices.
  • AUC dining discounts — present your AUC ID at participating locations.
  • AUC corporate hotel rates — check before booking hotels independently.
  • UNiDAYS — useful for discovering additional rotating student discounts.

Many of the most valuable benefits on this page depend on active student status. GitHub Education, Autodesk, Figma, Notion, discounted subscriptions and similar programs can require current enrollment or periodic re-verification.

AUC Library access is especially important to use before graduation because currently enrolled students receive substantially broader remote access to licensed research resources than alumni.

Whenever possible, verify and activate useful education benefits while your AUC student status and university email remain active.
