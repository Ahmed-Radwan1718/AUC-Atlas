Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026. This page explains what AUC Atlas may collect and how it may be used.

1. Information You Provide

AUC Atlas may collect information you submit, such as account details, professor reviews, course material metadata, uploaded files, professor names, semesters, course codes, support messages, and profile information.

2. Uploaded Files

Course materials uploaded to AUC Atlas may be stored with a file storage provider and tied to course, professor, semester, title, file type, file size, upload time, and moderation status.

3. Automatically Collected Data

The website, hosting provider, authentication tools, and storage providers may collect basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, device information, request logs, security events, and error logs.

4. How Information Is Used

  • To run course pages, professor pages, accounts, uploads, and GPA tools.
  • To organize uploaded material by course, professor, and semester.
  • To moderate unsafe, private, copyrighted, spam, or inaccurate content.
  • To protect the website from abuse, fraud, security threats, and technical errors.
  • To improve AUC Atlas features and reliability.

5. Sharing With Service Providers

AUC Atlas may use third-party services for hosting, authentication, file storage, databases, analytics, email, or security. These providers may process information only as needed to provide their services.

6. Public Content

Reviews, course material titles, professor names, semesters, course codes, and approved file links may become visible to other users. Do not upload personal, private, confidential, or identifying information that should not be public.

7. Cookies and Local Storage

AUC Atlas may use cookies, local storage, or session storage for login state, account features, security, preferences, and basic website functionality.

8. Data Removal

You may request review or removal of content you submitted or content that includes private, unsafe, copyrighted, or incorrect information. Some records may be kept when needed for security, moderation, legal, or technical reasons.

9. Student Reminder

Before uploading files, remove names, IDs, emails, grades, private messages, and any personal information that does not need to be shared.
