# Verified professor department assignments — 2026-11-12

This update fills previously missing `department`, `displayDepartment`, and `filterDepartment` for **65 professor records**. All existing names, emails, images, and other profile fields are unchanged.

## Method

- Preferred official American University in Cairo university profiles and research pages when available; otherwise cross-referenced **AUC-specific teaching/course listings** from Coursicle and matched the professor name (and existing AUC email when available).
- Followed AUC's [published course-code abbreviations](https://www.aucegypt.edu/student-life/first-year-program/common-abbreviations) and existing AUC Atlas labels.
- Music and graphic design teaching are assigned to the **Arts** department (both programs belong to Arts). Finance, marketing, and management-information-systems teaching are grouped under **Management** while preserving relevant display codes (FINC, MKTG, MOIS), in line with the academic units of the AUC School of Business.
- When the only publicly confirmed teaching unit is an interdisciplinary prefix (e.g., SCI, ENGR, SEMR, FYE), used a broader **Science**, **Engineering**, or **Core Curriculum** designation without inventing a narrower disciplinary affiliation.
- Some source pages show teaching areas rather than a formal employment department. A second faculty directory confirmation would improve those records.
- Names without sufficient corroboration remain `Department coming soon`; no speculative bulk-fill was performed.
